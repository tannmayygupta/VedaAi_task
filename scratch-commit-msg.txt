Fix Figma fidelity gaps: missing avatar icons, sidebar toggle, hero illustration

Prompted by the user spotting the top-right profile icon and the center
upload-screen illustration were missing, did a node-by-node comparison
against the actual Figma file and found four gaps in the shared shell:

- Header profile-avatar slot and sidebar school-logo slot both existed as
  empty placeholder divs instead of showing anything.
- Sidebar header's small toggle icon next to "VedaAI" was missing entirely.
- Header bell icon's notification dot was missing.
- The center decorative illustration on the Upload screen (concentric rings
  + orbiting icon badges) was missing entirely.

Filled the two avatar slots and the illustration's center with icons rather
than photos, since this no-auth/no-DB app has no real user or school image
to show - Figma's own assets there are placeholder photos, not real content.

Full reasoning and verification against the Figma screenshots in
docs/DECISIONS.md.
